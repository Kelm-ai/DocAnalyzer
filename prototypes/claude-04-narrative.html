This assessment evaluated the Information Security Management Policy v3.2 against 15 requirements from ISO 27001:2022. The document demonstrates strong compliance in areas of leadership commitment, policy documentation, and organizational roles. However, significant gaps were identified in risk assessment methodology, operational procedures, and measurable security objectives.
