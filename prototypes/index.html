Compliance Report — Design Prototypes

25 prototype variations exploring interaction patterns, information density, and layout for the compliance evaluation report. Each prototype addresses cognitive load reduction, AI trust signals, and the gap-first action model.

Design Principles Applied

  • 1 Gaps first — failures and flagged items surface before passes
  • 2 Evidence-led trust — every verdict is backed by document quotes visible on demand
  • 3 Progressive disclosure — summary → clause → requirement → evidence
  • 4 Human-in-the-loop — approve/reject/override every AI finding
  • 5 Confidence transparency — uncertainty is visible, not hidden
  • 6 Export-ready — every view can be shared with stakeholders
